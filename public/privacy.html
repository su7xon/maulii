Privacy Policy

Mauli Mobile · Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mauli Mobile ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Mauli Mobile app or the website at mauli-mobile.netlify.app. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details — name, phone number and email address you provide when you sign in or place an order.
  • Order information — products ordered, delivery address, pincode and order history.
  • Payment information — online payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card number, CVV or UPI credentials on our servers.
  • Device and usage data — basic technical data such as device type and pages visited, used to keep the app working and improve it.

2. How we use your information

  • To process and deliver your orders, and to contact you about them.
  • To provide customer support and handle returns or warranty requests.
  • To send order updates. We do not send marketing messages without your consent.
  • To detect and prevent fraud and misuse.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Delivery partners, so your order can reach you.
  • Payment processors (Razorpay) to complete a transaction.
  • Cloud hosting and database providers that run the app on our behalf.
  • Government or legal authorities, where the law requires it.

4. Data storage and security

Your data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. Access is limited to authorised staff. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS). No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

5. Your rights

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for optional communications at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

6. Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed to provide warranty and after-sales support, and to meet legal and accounting requirements. After that, data is deleted or anonymised.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Third-party links

The app may show links to other sites, such as brand or manufacturer pages. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
